## mpl-ocr

- Project: [mpl-ocr](https://github.com/Shonitbar/mpl-ocr)
- License: MIT
- Copyright (c) 2026 mpl-ocr contributors

MScout 0.6.0 uses mpl-ocr as a **reference** for the saturation-aware white-ink
key used on the level tile, the strict acceptance policy for learned glyphs, and
Windows OCR plus data-pack snapping for map labels. The implementation here is
written for MScout's capture and reader model. mpl-ocr is not a runtime
dependency, and no game assets from it are used.
